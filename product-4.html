Boutique pickup

Collect 11:00am – 3:00pm from the express bay at 256 High St, Kew. Need another time? Email info@frankandharri.com.au

Metro delivery

Refrigerated courier to eligible Melbourne postcodes, shown at checkout. $15, free over $150.

Getting it home

Carry flat on the car floor with the air conditioning on. Refrigerate, then bring out 1-2 hours before cutting.

Pure Belgian Chocolate

Crafted exclusively with Callebaut 70% dark chocolate callets, cultured Dutch cocoa powder, and European unsalted butter.

Contains Dairy, Gluten & Eggs

Contains wheat flour, free-range Gippsland eggs, and cultured buttermilk. Made in an atelier that processes tree nuts and sesame.

Gluten-Free Atelier Option

Looking for 100% gluten-free? Our Flourless Orange & Almond Cake and Gluten-Free Chocolate Gateau are prepared in a dedicated allergen protocol.
