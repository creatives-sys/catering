FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know Before Ordering

Helpful guidance on delivery, shelf life, transportation, and custom celebration plaques.

Transport & Handling How should I transport the cake if collecting from Kew?

Always place your cake box flat on the front passenger floor of your vehicle, never on an angled car seat or in a warm boot. Put your vehicle's air conditioning on high. Once home, immediately transfer the box into your refrigerator below 4°C until 45 minutes prior to slicing.

💡 Pâtissier Tip: We provide sturdy, presentation-grade transport boxes with reinforced bases so your cake stays immaculate during car journeys.
Bakehouse Schedule How far in advance do I need to place my order?

We require a minimum of 72 hours advance notice for celebration cakes to slow-temper our chocolate ganaches and bake our fresh sponge layers. If you need a cake sooner, call our Kew boutique directly at (03) 9853 0128 to check today's bakehouse capacity.

Hand-Piped Plaque Can I customise the chocolate message plaque?

Yes! Every celebration cake includes a complimentary hand-piped dark chocolate plaque. In the options section above, simply tick the chocolate plaque checkbox to type your custom inscription (up to 32 characters) or select presets like "Happy Birthday!", "Congratulations!" or choose no plaque.

Freshness & Storage How long will the cake stay fresh after collection?

Our cakes are best enjoyed within 4 days of collection when stored in their original presentation box in the refrigerator. We recommend removing the cake 45 minutes before serving so the Lescure French butter and Belgian chocolate ganache reach a silky, melting room-temperature texture.

Melbourne Delivery What are your Melbourne delivery areas and fees?

We deliver across Melbourne Metro via dedicated refrigerated courier vans. Delivery is Complimentary on orders over $150, and a flat $15.00 for orders under $150. You can also collect for free from our Kew boutique at 259 High Street with convenient 15-minute collection parking right outside.
