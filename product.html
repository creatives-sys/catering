The art of Creation

Crafted daily in our Kew boutique atelier using cultured Lescure French butter and single-origin Belgian chocolate. Five whisper-thin buttermilk sponge layers bound by slow-tempered chocolate fudge, Madagascar bourbon vanilla, and hand-harvested Maldon sea-salt flakes.

Allergens & Dietary Transparency

Contains gluten, dairy, egg. Facility handles tree nuts, sesame, and soy. Dedicated gluten-free preparations are available across our flourless citrus tortes, almond ricotta cakes, and San Sebastián burnt cheesecake collection.

Preservation & serving

Serving Ritual: Bring cake to room temperature 45 minutes prior to slicing for a silky, melting ganache texture. Dip a long thin knife in hot water and wipe dry between each cut.

Storage: Keep refrigerated below 4°C in our presentation box. Best enjoyed within 4 days of collection.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know Before Ordering

Helpful guidance on delivery, shelf life, transportation, and custom celebration plaques.

How should I transport the cake if collecting from Kew? +

Always place your cake box flat on the front passenger floor of your vehicle, never on an angled car seat or in a warm boot. Put your vehicle's air conditioning on high. Once home, immediately transfer the box into your refrigerator below 4°C until 45 minutes prior to slicing.

How far in advance do I need to place my order? +

We require a minimum of 72 hours advance notice for celebration cakes to slow-temper our chocolate ganaches and bake our fresh sponge layers. If you need a cake sooner, call our Kew boutique directly at (03) 9853 0128 to check today's bakehouse capacity.

Can I customise the chocolate message plaque? +

Yes! Every celebration cake includes a complimentary hand-piped dark chocolate plaque. In Step 3 of the configurator above, you can type your custom message (up to 32 characters) or select presets like "Happy Birthday!", "Congratulations!" or choose the Classic Atelier finish with no plaque.

How long will the cake stay fresh after collection? +

Our cakes are best enjoyed within 4 days of collection when stored in their original presentation box in the refrigerator. We recommend removing the cake 45 minutes before serving so the Lescure French butter and Belgian chocolate ganache reach a silky, melting room-temperature texture.

What are your Melbourne delivery areas and fees? +

We deliver across Melbourne Metro via dedicated refrigerated courier vans. Delivery is Complimentary on orders over $150, and a flat $15.00 for orders under $150. You can also collect for free from our Kew boutique at 259 High Street with convenient 15-minute collection parking right outside.
