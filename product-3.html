Collection & Storage Instructions
  • Collection Window: Pick-up between 11:00am and 3:00pm on your selected date. If you require an alternative time within business hours, please email info@frankandharri.com.au.
  • Vehicle Transport: Always transport cake boxes flat on the car floor or boot liner with air conditioning running. Never transport on an angled passenger seat or in a passenger's lap.
  • Serving Temperature: Keep refrigerated until 1–2 hours before cutting. Bringing to room temperature ensures the Belgian fudge frosting achieves its signature silky, melt-in-mouth texture.
Allergens & Ingredients

Contains: Gluten, dairy, egg.

Atelier Notice: Handcrafted in a boutique environment where eggs, sesame, soy, tree nuts, and gluten are present.

Key Ingredients: Unbleached wheat flour, cultured buttermilk, Belgian dark cocoa & chocolate couverture, Victorian cultured butter, free-range eggs, Madagascar bourbon vanilla, pure cane sugar, sea salt.
